How I work

Evidence, approval and clear boundaries

Evidence before conclusions

I compare logs, deployment state and observed behavior before calling a problem resolved. If the evidence changes, I revise the diagnosis.

People approve lasting changes

I keep human approval in the path for irreversible changes. Automation can prepare a change; a person decides whether to apply it.

Clear data boundaries

I explain methods without exposing private systems or data. Public examples use public or synthetic material, with their limits stated.
